Philippine Red Cross

Join our mission to provide life-saving services and alleviate human suffering through volunteerism with the Philippine Red Cross.

About Philippine Red Cross

Established in 1947, the Philippine Red Cross (PRC) is the premier humanitarian organization in the Philippines, dedicated to delivering quality life-saving services, disaster response, and social welfare programs. With a strong network of volunteers, PRC responds to emergencies, promotes health, and supports vulnerable communities nationwide.

Volunteer Roles for Students

  • Blood Drive Volunteer: Assist in organizing and managing blood donation drives, including donor registration and promotion of the Pledge 25 program for youth aged 18–25.
  • Disaster Response Support: Provide logistical support during disaster relief operations, such as distributing aid and assembling relief kits.
  • Junior First Aider: Learn and apply basic first aid skills in school clinics or community events, supporting the Junior First Aiders Program.
  • Red Cross Youth Leader: Participate in or lead Red Cross Youth (RCY) councils to organize community service projects and promote humanitarian values.

Benefits of Volunteering

  • Develop leadership, communication, and teamwork skills through hands-on humanitarian work.
  • Receive free training in first aid, disaster preparedness, and other life-saving skills.
  • Earn a Certificate of Volunteering after completing 300 hours of service, enhancing college applications.
  • Contribute to community resilience and make a meaningful impact on vulnerable populations.
  • Build connections with a network of dedicated volunteers and humanitarian professionals.

Requirements to Volunteer

  • Age: High school students aged 13–18 (parental consent required for those under 18).
  • Commitment: Flexible, based on availability; typically weekly or monthly involvement.
  • Training: Complete the Basic Volunteer Orientation Course (BVOC).
  • Documents: Submit PRC Membership (MAAB), two valid IDs, resume, and 2x2 ID picture.
  • Location: Opportunities available at local chapters or virtual roles.
