Project Vision: Students Opportunities Hub
Context: High school students often struggle to find extracurricular activities, internships, and jobs that align with their interests due to fragmented information sources. The intended audience is high school students aged 14–18, their parents, and school counselors in the U.S.
Role: The project team, including developers, designers, and educators, will create a centralized platform to empower students.
Axioms:

Accessibility: The platform must be free and easy to use.
Relevance: Opportunities must be tailored to local communities and student interests.
Empowerment: Enable students to take charge of their personal and professional growth.

Framework:

Build a user-friendly, mobile-responsive website.
Partner with schools and local organizations to populate opportunities.
Use a recommendation engine to match students with opportunities.

Task: Develop a vision for a digital marketplace that connects high school students with extracurricular and professional opportunities.
Examples/Exemplars:

LinkedIn for professional networking.
Handshake for college internships.
Local school bulletin boards for club advertisements.

Deliverables:

A vision statement document outlining the platform’s purpose and impact.
Format: Markdown file shared with stakeholders.
